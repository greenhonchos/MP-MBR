11 · GH Insights

GH Insights — Closing Read-out

What worked, what hurt, where to recover & the 90-day operating plan
◆ GH Insight · Boardroom Synthesis
Feb 2025 was a healthy top-line month (+18.4% gross) — but ~₹5.18 L of recoverable revenue is leaking through returns & RTO.
Two levers drive ~85% of the leakage: Flipkart RTO (57.7%) and Sizing-led customer returns (71.7% of all CRs). Fixing both lifts blended return % from 42.04% → ~33.5%, recovering an estimated ₹1.5 L per month with no incremental ad spend.

What's Working
Defend & scale these moats
Strengths
01Ajio is best-in-class
Return % of 16.67% vs blended 42.04% — paid users convert & retain. Net Qty 60 of 72 shipped.
02Tatacliq · zero-return month
22 of 22 units retained · 100% net realization. Channel is small but profitable — ready to scale.
03Myntra ASP discipline
₹1,561 ASP — highest of all channels at the largest volume (328 units, 41.6% rev share).
04Kurta demand-supply fit
76.2% of qty sold & ₹9.17 L revenue. Healthy depth (190 articles ≥3 units).
05Discount discipline
99% of sales within the 0–10% discount band. ASP not eroded by markdowns.
06SLA compliance ~96%
Forward-logistics on-time across all 5 channels — no fulfillment penalty exposure.
What's Hurting
Critical leakage points
Issues
01Flipkart RTO crisis
57.72% RTO — 86 of 149 returns courier-rejected. Single largest leak: ~₹1.78 L lost net revenue this month.
02Sizing-led customer returns
134 of 187 CRs (71.7%) attributed to sizing. Catalog & size-chart problem, not product quality.
03Amazon CPU is poor
53.16% return % — worst per-unit performance. Only 37 net qty from 79 shipped.
04Shirt return rate elevated
51.35% vs Kurta 40.2%. Fit consistency or category-fit on platform off-target.
05Dead inventory drag
79 articles OOS (demand lost) + top slow-movers hold 276 / 237 / 155 units with >35% return rates.
06Hero SKU bleeding
108-99258-43 — 78 sold but 35.9% return. Best-seller doubling as biggest single leak.
Scope of Improvement
High-leverage interventions
Opportunities
01Refresh Kurta & Shirt size charts
Re-shoot fit on real models; publish standardized cm-grids on Myntra + Flipkart. Est. −5.4 pp blended return %.
02COD throttle on Flipkart Tier-3 pincodes
Whitelist proven-deliver pincodes. Est. RTO 57.7% → ~38%.
03Top-20 SKU PDP overhaul
Add fit videos + first-3-star review prioritisation + measurements per size for hero SKUs.
04Scale Ajio + Tatacliq
Tatacliq has 0% returns; Ajio at 16.67%. Re-allocate 20% of Flipkart paid budget here. +₹1.2 L expected gross.
05Markdown plan for slow movers
Time-boxed clearance on 8 SKUs holding 1,200+ units. Free working capital before AW25 buys.
06Restructure Amazon
Pause poor-ROAS SKUs; A+ content for kept assortment; pincode-level COD restriction.
